Your Etisalat SIM Is Suspended. Here Is the Clock You Are On

Suspension is not the end of the line, but it starts a three month countdown that most people do not know exists. After it runs out the operator is entitled to give your number to somebody else. Here is the exact sequence.

Short answer

How long do you have before a suspended Etisalat number is gone for good? Three months from the date of suspension. If you submit the required registration information inside that window, the operator has to lift the suspension, restore service and renew your registration. If you do not, it deactivates the line, and after deactivation it is entitled to reuse your number and is under no obligation to give it back to you.

Why this happens, and why it hits expats hardest

Your SIM registration has an expiry date. For UAE nationals it tracks the Emirates ID. For everyone else, the registration expiry is set to the expiry of the residence visa or the passport, whichever comes sooner. That is the detail that catches people out. You can be holding a perfectly valid Emirates ID and still have a registration that expired, because the passport underneath it ran out first.

There is one softener written into the rules. If the identity document you register with expires less than a year from the day you register, the operator is allowed to set your registration expiry a full year out instead. So the date on your registration is not always the date on your visa.

The part worth reading twice. Suspension is reversible. Deactivation is not, as a matter of right. Once a line is deactivated the operator is entitled to reuse the number without further notice to you, and it is under no obligation to reissue it. If the number has any value to you, personal or commercial, the three month window is the whole game.

Suspended is not the same as deactivated

People use the words interchangeably and then panic unnecessarily, or fail to panic when they should. A suspended line can still receive calls and receive SMS. What stops is outgoing calls, other than emergency numbers and the operator. That is why a suspended line often goes unnoticed for weeks, especially on a second line that mostly receives verification codes. A deactivated line does nothing at all, and the number behind it goes back into the pool.

Renewing your registration costs nothing

Worth stating plainly, because it stops people delaying. The rules prohibit operators from imposing any new or additional charge for registration or for registration renewal. If you are putting off updating your Emirates ID details because you assume there is a fee attached, there is not one for the registration itself.

The operator also has to run a free consumer care line for exactly these enquiries, and has to notify you of the outcome of a registration within 48 hours.

Frequently asked questions

How long before a suspended Etisalat number is permanently lost?

Three months from the date of suspension. Submit the required registration information within that window and the operator has to revoke the suspension, reinstate the service and renew your registration. After three months it deactivates the line.

Why was my Etisalat SIM suspended?

Almost always because the SIM registration expired. For residents who are not UAE or GCC nationals the registration expiry follows the residence visa or the passport, whichever comes sooner, so an expired passport can suspend a line even while the Emirates ID is still valid. Submitting false registration information is the other cause.

Can I still receive calls on a suspended Etisalat line?

Yes. A suspended line can still receive calls and receive SMS. What stops is outgoing calls, other than emergency numbers and the operator itself. That is why suspension on a second line often goes unnoticed.

Will Etisalat warn me before suspending my number?

It has to. No less than one calendar month before your registration expires the operator must tell you the registration is expiring, give you the date, and warn that the SIM will be suspended unless you renew. It must also give notice of the suspension itself within 24 hours, and a further notice two weeks before the three month deadline.

Does it cost anything to renew my SIM registration?

No. The rules prohibit operators from imposing any new or additional charges for registration or registration renewal.

Can I get my number back after it has been deactivated?

You can ask, but the operator is not obliged to agree. After deactivation it is under no obligation to accept further registration information from you, it is entitled to reuse the number without further notice, and it is under no obligation to reissue the number to you.

What do I need to reactivate a suspended Etisalat SIM?

Valid identity documents, submitted before the three month deadline. Take originals, because the operator has to compare the original against the copy, and it is then required to return the originals to you and retain only copies.

How do I check whether my other numbers are about to be suspended too?

Every operator has to provide a free confidential service showing the registration details of any SIM registered to you. The regulator also runs Hesabati, which shows the mobile and fixed line numbers registered under your Emirates ID across UAE providers, free, through its website using UAE Pass.
