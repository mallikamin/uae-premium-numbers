Why the cheapest single plan isn't the best for families

The instinct for a family of 3-5 is usually "buy 5 of the cheapest plans." That works (5 × AED 188 = AED 940/month) but loses three things:

  • No unlimited international calling — basic plans are local-only or flexi-limited. Parents calling back home rack up overages.
  • No roaming data — if the family travels, someone's phone becomes useless on arrival.
  • No premium number — no Gold for dad/primary contact, which matters for business cards, delivery apps, school records.

The smarter structure: one primary plan that covers "family edge cases" (international, roaming, VIP number) + cheaper plans for everyone else.

When to upgrade the primary plan

Move the primary from Gold 500 (AED 500) to Gold 500 Local (AED 500) only if you don't need international calling. Same price, local minutes only. Most UAE families with roots abroad (Pakistani, Indian, Filipino, Egyptian) should stay on Flexi for international.

Move primary to Platinum (AED 1,000) if:

  • The family travels internationally 2+ times/year (20GB roaming included covers most trips)
  • You need a Platinum VIP number for business image
  • The primary user is a high-volume international caller (unlimited flexi minutes)

What about children's phones?

For kids under 16 needing only WhatsApp + maps + location tracking:

  • Etisalat doesn't offer a "kids" tier. Cheapest postpaid is AED 188.
  • Alternative: Get them on prepaid — Etisalat prepaid starts at AED 50/month. Use a family data pack (AED 75 for 10GB).
  • Share location via Google/Apple family sharing; connect them to home Wi-Fi for most usage.

eSIM + shared devices tip
