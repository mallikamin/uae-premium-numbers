The Best Etisalat Plan for Your Family

Etisalat does not sell one family plan. A family is several ordinary postpaid lines, and the best setup depends on what your household actually does. Choose the head's plan around the family's main need, then give everyone else the cheapest plan that covers them. Four common households, priced line by line.

Short answer

What is the best Etisalat plan for a family? There is no single one. The setup that works is one stronger plan for the family head, chosen for the household's main need, with everyone else on Plan 250 at AED 188. A family that calls home daily puts the head on Unlimited 1 Country 325. One that travels uses New Freedom 375. A head who runs a business takes Gold Plan 500 for the Gold number. The four households below run from AED 701 to AED 980 a month before VAT.

Start with the head's plan

Etisalat's family arrangement links ordinary lines under one primary account. It needs the head on a Freedom plan of AED 200 or more, and it gives the group a shared 10 GB of local data each month. The rules in full are in our guide to family mobile plans. To stay clear of that floor, every head plan in the examples below costs more than AED 200 even at the discounted monthly price.

The head's line is where the family's special needs belong: the unlimited calling abroad, the roaming, the premium number. Everyone else rarely needs more than the AED 188 plan, and paying for five mid-tier plans is the most common way households overspend.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Etisalat plan for a family?

There is no single family plan. The best setup is one stronger plan for the family head, chosen for the household's main need, with the other members on Plan 250 at AED 188. Unlimited 1 Country 325 suits families who call home daily, New Freedom 375 suits families who travel, and Gold Plan 500 suits a head who wants a Gold number for business.

How much does an Etisalat family plan cost a month?

The four households on this page run from AED 701 for three lines to AED 980 for five, before 5 percent VAT. The head's plan is the biggest variable; every other line is usually AED 188.

Which plan should the head of the family be on?

A Freedom plan of AED 200 or more, since the family arrangement requires it, and the one that covers the family's main need: unlimited calls to one country, roaming data, or a Gold number.

Should children be on the cheapest Etisalat plan?

Usually yes. Plan 250 Local at AED 188 gives non-stop data at a capped speed, so a child's line slows down instead of running up an extra charge, and 1,000 local minutes covers school and family calls.

Can every family member get a free number?

Yes. Each line includes a free number in its plan's tier, Silver on most plans and Gold on the Gold plans. We can look for a matching set with the same ending, subject to what is in the catalogue.

Can we keep the numbers we already have?

Yes. Numbers on du can be ported to Etisalat, and existing Etisalat prepaid numbers can move to a postpaid plan without changing.
