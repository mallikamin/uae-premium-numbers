Nationals and expats: the two differences that matter

Plan range. Everyone can take any of the 22 Freedom postpaid plans, from AED 188 to AED 1,250 a month. UAE nationals can also take e&'s Emirati plans, which are reserved for them. What those include and how they compare is on our Etisalat plans for UAE nationals page.

Family size. Etisalat's family arrangement lets a UAE national link up to 10 lines under one head, against 5 for an expat. The head needs a Freedom plan of AED 200 or more and the group shares 10 GB of local data a month. The rest of the rules are in our family mobile plans guide.
