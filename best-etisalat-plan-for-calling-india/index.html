Unlimited 1 Country 325: the plan built for this

You nominate India as your preferred country when the line is activated, and from then on every call to a India mobile or landline is unlimited. The 700 flexi minutes stay untouched for UAE calls and for the occasional call elsewhere. At AED 325 a month that is a little under AED 11 a day for unlimited calling home, with 27 GB of data on top. For the Indian community in the UAE this is the plan we activate most, and it is the one to compare everything else against.

Unlimited Calls 600 Flexi: for more than one country

If your calls go to India and also to relatives or suppliers elsewhere, the extra AED 35 a month is worth it. Every flexi minute is unlimited, local and international, so you stop counting altogether. It also carries 50 GB of data and 2 GB of roaming, which covers a short trip without a local SIM. The list price is AED 600; the AED 360 is the current promotional price and has held all year.

New Freedom 260 Flexi: the budget route

AED 195 a month buys 600 flexi minutes plus unlimited calls to one preferred number of your choice. If the calls that matter go to one India number, a parent or a partner, nominate that number and those calls are unlimited; the 600 minutes cover the rest. If you call several people at length, you will run through 600 minutes in a fortnight and the 325 plan is the better buy.

Local Data & Calls 1200 Flexi: nothing to think about

Unlimited data at full speed, unlimited flexi minutes to anywhere, 40 GB of roaming. At the current AED 600 it is half its list price, and for someone who travels to India regularly the 40 GB of roaming replaces a local SIM on every trip.

Regular calls or WhatsApp calls

Most people use both. WhatsApp calls cost data and depend on the connection at both ends, which in many towns in India is the weak link. A regular call on the plan's minutes uses the phone network and holds up anywhere. The sensible split is WhatsApp for the daily chat and plan minutes for the calls that cannot drop: parents, business, anything urgent. With unlimited India calling the distinction stops mattering.

Dialling India from your Etisalat line

Dial 00 91, or +91, then the ten-digit mobile number. For a landline add the city code after 91, so a Mumbai landline is +91 22 followed by the number. On the Unlimited 1 Country plan with India nominated, both are unlimited.
