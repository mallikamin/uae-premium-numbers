Short answer

What do you need to get an Etisalat postpaid SIM in the UAE? Your original Emirates ID, plus one supporting document from e&'s accepted list. The supporting document can be a stamped salary certificate, a Ministry of Labour contract, a vehicle licence, a three month bank statement, last month's utility bill showing your physical address, or a tenancy or ownership contract. Where a salary is used as the proof, the stated minimum is AED 2,500.

Almost everything written about this online is guesswork. The list below is e&'s own published requirement for subscribing to a postpaid mobile plan, not a summary of what someone remembers happening at a store.

The two things you need

1. Original Emirates ID, mandatory

Not a photocopy, not a photo on your phone at the counter. The original. It has to be valid, not expired. If your Emirates ID is still being processed, your residency file is not complete yet and a postpaid line cannot be opened against it.

2. One supporting document, your choice from this list

You supply one of the six, not all six. Which one you pick makes no difference to the plan or the price.

If your salary is under AED 2,500

The AED 2,500 figure is attached to the two salary based documents, the stamped salary certificate and the Ministry of Labour contract. It is a condition on those documents, not a blanket eligibility test on you. The other four accepted documents carry no salary condition at all. So if your salary sits below the line, or you are paid in a way that a certificate does not capture cleanly, use one of these instead:

  • A three month bank statement, which is the usual route for freelancers, partners and business owners.
  • Your tenancy contract.
  • Your vehicle licence.
  • Last month's utility bill in your name.

This is the single most useful thing on this page and it is the part people get wrong. Being under AED 2,500 does not disqualify you from postpaid. It only closes off two of the six proof routes.

Visitors and people on a visit visa

Postpaid subscription is built around holding a valid Emirates ID, which means residency. If you are in the UAE on a visit or tourist visa and do not hold an Emirates ID, postpaid is not the product for you. Prepaid on a passport is, and you can convert that line to postpaid later once your residency and Emirates ID are issued, keeping the same number. See how the prepaid to postpaid switch works.

Your credit limit is set after verification, not before

Postpaid accounts carry a monthly cap, sometimes called a credit limit, and it is set once your account has been verified during the purchase. It is not a fixed number published in advance and it is not a deposit you pay up front. If your usage pattern later justifies a higher cap you can ask for it to be reviewed.

Keeping your existing number

You do not have to accept a new number. Three situations, three answers:

  • You already have an Etisalat prepaid line. It can be converted to postpaid and the number stays with you.
  • You are with another UAE operator. Mobile number portability lets you bring the number across. Bring the same documents plus the number you want ported.
  • You want a better number. Every plan includes a free number from a tier, Silver on plans up to AED 360, Gold on the AED 500 plans, Platinum on AED 1,000. You choose it from the available list rather than being assigned one.

Frequently asked questions

What documents are required for an Etisalat postpaid SIM in the UAE?

Your original Emirates ID plus one supporting document. The accepted supporting documents are a stamped salary certificate, a Ministry of Labour contract, a vehicle licence, a three month bank or online bank statement, last month's utility bill showing your physical address, or a tenancy or ownership contract.

Is there a minimum salary for an Etisalat postpaid plan?

AED 2,500 is the minimum stated where a stamped salary certificate or a Ministry of Labour contract is used as the supporting document. It is a condition on those two documents, not a blanket rule. The other four accepted documents carry no salary condition.

Can I get a postpaid SIM without a salary certificate?

Yes. Use one of the other accepted documents instead: a three month bank statement, a tenancy or ownership contract, last month's utility bill in your name, or a vehicle licence. Any one of them satisfies the requirement on its own.

Can I get an Etisalat postpaid SIM on a visit visa?

No. Postpaid subscription requires a valid original Emirates ID, which means residency. On a visit or tourist visa, take a prepaid line on your passport. Once your Emirates ID is issued you can convert that same number to postpaid.

Do I need to pay a deposit for a postpaid line?

e&'s published subscription requirements do not list a deposit. Postpaid accounts carry a monthly cap that is set after your account is verified during the purchase, rather than an upfront payment.

Can I keep my current mobile number when I take a postpaid plan?

Yes. An existing Etisalat prepaid line can be converted to postpaid with the number intact, and a number on another UAE operator can be brought across through mobile number portability. Bring the same documents either way.

Do I have to go to an Etisalat store?

No. Through an authorized dealer you send your Emirates ID and one supporting document on WhatsApp, choose your number, and the SIM is delivered. Same day in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah for orders before 4 PM.

Is a photocopy of my Emirates ID enough?

The requirement specifies the original Emirates ID. For a dealer order you send a clear image of it for the application and the original is verified at delivery.
