Local minutes or flexi minutes, the choice people get wrong

Nearly every plan above comes in two versions and the only difference is the minute bundle.

  • Local gives you more minutes, usable only inside the UAE. Plan 250 Local has 1,000 of them.
  • Flexi gives you fewer minutes that work both inside the UAE and for international calls. Plan 250 Flexi has 500.

The rule of thumb: if you call home more than about twice a week, take Flexi and accept the smaller bundle. If your calls are all UAE numbers, take Local and get double the minutes for the same money. Getting this wrong is the most common reason a UAE postpaid bill comes in higher than expected, because out of bundle international minutes are charged per minute.

What is not in the advertised price

  • 5 percent VAT is added to every line. AED 188 bills at AED 197.40. AED 300 bills at AED 315.
  • Roaming beyond the bundle is charged separately once your included roaming data is used.
  • Out of bundle minutes, mainly international calls on a Local plan.
  • Handset instalments, if you take a device with the plan, appear as a separate line on the same bill.

What is genuinely included at no charge through this dealer route: the SIM, the delivery, the activation, and the premium number tier shown against each plan.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest postpaid mobile plan in the UAE?

AED 188 a month before VAT, which is Freedom Plan 250 at the authorized dealer price against a list price of AED 250. It includes non-stop data at up to 3 Mbps, 1,000 local minutes and a free Silver number. With 5 percent VAT the bill is AED 197.40.

How many Etisalat postpaid plans are there?

22 in the current catalogue, spread across three families: Freedom Non-Stop Data, Freedom Unlimited Data, and Freedom Unlimited Calling. Most exist in a Local and a Flexi version, which differ only in the minute bundle.

What is the difference between Local and Flexi minutes?

Local minutes work only for UAE numbers and you get more of them. Flexi minutes work for both UAE and international calls and you get fewer. On Plan 250 it is 1,000 local against 500 flexi for the same AED 188.

At what price does the data cap come off?

AED 300. Everything from AED 188 to AED 263 gives you non-stop data at a capped speed, from 3 Mbps up to 50 Mbps. The Unlimited Data 500 plan at AED 300 is the first tier with data at maximum speed and no cap, and it adds 10 GB of roaming.

Is a postpaid plan in the UAE a monthly charge or a one time payment?

Monthly. Every plan listed is postpaid, meaning you use the service and are billed at the end of each cycle. There is no one time purchase version of these plans.

Is there a contract, and how long?

Postpaid plans normally carry a 12 month commitment. If you end it early, e& applies an exit charge equal to one month of the plan or AED 1,000, whichever is lower. Confirm the exact terms for your plan before signing.

Do I have to pay extra for the premium number?

No. The number tier shown against each plan is included. Plans up to AED 360 include a Silver number, the AED 500 Gold plans include a Gold number, and the AED 1,000 Platinum plans include a Platinum number. You can pay separately for a specific number outside your tier if you want one.

Yes. Send your documents on WhatsApp, choose a number, and the SIM is delivered to you with card payment on delivery. Same day across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah for orders before 4 PM.
