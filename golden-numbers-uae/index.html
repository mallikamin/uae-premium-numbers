The patterns that make a number golden

Scarcity is decided by the digits, and the digits are easy to read once you know the families:

7777 · 9999Quads. One digit four times at the end. Platinum territory.
1234 · 4567Straight runs, up or down. Usually Gold.
4747 · 2828Repeated pairs. The classic Silver pattern.
1221 · 5885Mirrors that read the same both ways. Gold.
786 · 999Endings with cultural or lucky weight. Requested constantly.
5050 · 0505Two-digit numbers, where the whole line uses only two digits. Platinum.

Two things push a number up a tier: fewer distinct digits, and the pattern sitting at the end where people say it out loud. A 050 or 054 or 056 prefix does not change the tier; the last four to six digits do.
