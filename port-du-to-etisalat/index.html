What portability is, in three lines

Mobile number portability lets a UAE subscriber move a number between du and Etisalat. The regulator mandates it, so neither operator can charge for the port itself. It works for prepaid and postpaid numbers, and your du service keeps working until the moment the switch completes.

Before you start: the five checks

  • Emirates ID, valid, front and back. The new Etisalat line is registered to it.
  • The du account is active, not suspended or disconnected.
  • No unpaid du bills. An outstanding balance is the most common rejection.
  • No live du contract with early-termination fees, or a decision to pay them.
  • The number is at least 90 days old on du. Newer numbers have to wait out the period.

Dial *135# on the du line to see the account status, or call du on 155 and ask whether anything blocks a port. Five minutes here saves a week later.

The process

  1. Choose the Etisalat plan. The number lands on the plan you pick. The plan index lists all 22; if you call one country a lot, start with the Unlimited 1 Country plan.
  2. Send us the request on WhatsApp: du number, Emirates ID, plan. We submit the portability request to Etisalat on your behalf.
  3. Verification. Etisalat and the regulator check the account is in good standing and the number is eligible. This is where an unpaid bill or a live contract stops the port.
  4. SIM delivery. We deliver the Etisalat SIM free anywhere in the UAE, usually the same day, already provisioned for your number.
  5. The switch. Within two to four business days you get an SMS; insert the Etisalat SIM, restart the phone, and the number is live on Etisalat.

What happens to WhatsApp, banking and OTPs

Nothing. Those services are tied to the number, and the number does not change. WhatsApp keeps its chats, your bank keeps sending OTPs to the same number, and nobody in your contacts has to do anything. During the 15 to 30 minute switch window you may miss a call or an SMS; after the restart everything resumes.

Why people move

  • Coverage where they actually are. Etisalat's 5G and highway footprint is wider outside Dubai and Abu Dhabi. See the full comparison.
  • A plan du does not sell. Unlimited calls to one country for AED 325, or unlimited data with international minutes for AED 600.
  • The number. Porting keeps your existing number. If you want a golden number instead, that is a new Etisalat line; many customers keep the ported number for family and take a golden number for business.

When a port fails, and what to do

  • Rejected for an outstanding balance or contract. Settle with du on 155, then we resubmit. Ask du about early termination if a contract is live.
  • Number under 90 days. Wait out the period; we can hold your plan choice and number in the meantime.
  • Taking longer than five business days. Message us and we escalate with Etisalat. Holidays are the usual cause.
  • Missed calls at the switch. Normal for 15 to 30 minutes. If it lasts longer, restart the phone with the Etisalat SIM and toggle airplane mode once.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to port from du to Etisalat?

Two to four business days from the request, most often three. Weekends and public holidays can add a day or two. The actual switch causes a 15 to 30 minute gap in service.

Does porting from du to Etisalat cost anything?

No. The port itself is free by regulation. You pay only the Etisalat plan the number moves onto. Any early-termination fee on a live du contract is between you and du.

Can I port a prepaid du number?

Yes. Portability covers prepaid and postpaid numbers. The number must be active, paid up and at least 90 days old.

Will I lose WhatsApp or bank OTPs when I port?

No. Those services follow the number, and the number does not change. You may miss messages during the 15 to 30 minute switch window only.

Why was my port request rejected?

Almost always an unpaid du bill, a live du contract, or a number younger than 90 days. Clear the balance or contract with du on 155, or wait out the 90 days, and we resubmit.

Can I get a golden number and keep my du number?

Yes, as two lines: the ported number stays yours on its Etisalat plan, and a golden number comes as a new Etisalat line with its own plan. Many customers keep one for family and one for business.
