International calling

Etisalat sells an Unlimited 1 Country plan at AED 325: unlimited calls to one country you nominate, India, Pakistan, the Philippines, anywhere on the list, with 700 flexi minutes and 27 GB besides. du has no equivalent; international minutes are add-ons. Frequently asked questions

Is Etisalat better than du in the UAE?

For most residents in 2026, yes: wider 5G coverage outside the main cities, unlimited data from AED 188 where du caps it, a free golden number on every plan, an unlimited-to-one-country calling plan du does not offer, and more roaming data at every tier. du wins on its Entertainer bundle and on shorter commitments.

Which is cheaper, Etisalat or du?

At the entry level Etisalat: Plan 250 Local is AED 188 a month against du Power 200 at AED 200, and it includes unlimited data and 1,000 minutes. At AED 500 they match on price and differ on what is included.

Which has better coverage, Etisalat or du?

In Dubai and Abu Dhabi both are excellent. Outside them, Etisalat's 5G and highway coverage is wider, which matters if you commute between emirates or work on sites.

Can I get unlimited international calls with Etisalat?

Yes. Unlimited 1 Country 325 gives unlimited calls to one nominated country for AED 325 a month, and Unlimited Calls 600 Flexi gives unlimited flexi minutes to every supported country for AED 360.

How do I tell an Etisalat number from a du number?

By the prefix. Etisalat numbers start with 050, 054 and 056; du numbers with 052, 055 and 058. Ported numbers keep their original prefix, so the rule is a strong hint rather than a guarantee.
