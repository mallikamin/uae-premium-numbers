Silver vs Gold vs Platinum Numbers

Etisalat grades every memorable number into one of three tiers by the scarcity of its pattern, and each tier is issued with a specific postpaid plan. This guide shows what separates them so you pick the tier, then the number.

Short answer

What is the difference between Silver, Gold and Platinum numbers? The pattern. Silver is repeated pairs and simple endings, free with plans from AED 188 a month. Gold is straight runs and mirrors, issued with the AED 500 a month plan. Platinum is triples, quads and lines built from one or two digits, issued with the AED 1,000 a month plan. Rarer pattern, higher tier, bigger plan.

Silver: the tier most people should start with

Silver holds the numbers that are clearly better than a random allocation without being scarce: a repeated pair at the end, a double-zero, a short run in the last three digits. They are plentiful, which is exactly why they are free with the entry plan. The practical test is whether someone can repeat the number back after hearing it once. A Silver ending passes that test. For a personal line, a delivery business or a first business card, this is the tier that makes sense, and the AED 188 plan it rides on is the cheapest postpaid plan Etisalat sells.

Gold: the business tier

Gold patterns are the ones people notice on a shop sign or a vehicle: 1234, 5678, 1221, 9988. They are visibly ordered, and there are far fewer of them. Gold is issued with the AED 500 plan, which brings 3,000 local minutes, so it is also the tier that suits a line that takes calls all day. Brokers, consultants, clinics and trading companies make up most of our Gold buyers. If you already spend around AED 500 a month on a business line, a Gold number costs you nothing extra.

Platinum: the scarce tier

Platinum is where scarcity becomes visible in the arithmetic. A quad ending like 7777 exists in a handful of combinations per prefix; a line built from only two digits, fewer still. These numbers are attached to the AED 1,000 plan with unlimited data and unlimited local calls. A small number of exceptional Platinum lines carry a one-time premium on top of the plan, quoted per number before you commit. Once a Platinum number is allocated it is off the market for as long as the owner keeps the line.

How to tell which tier a number is

  • Count distinct digits in the last four. Four the same is Platinum. Two alternating (4747) is Silver. An ordered run of four (1234) is Gold.
  • Look at position. The tier is decided by the subscriber part, the seven digits after 050, 054 or 056. The prefix never changes the tier.
  • Check the catalogue. Every number in the live search is labelled with its tier and the plan it comes with, so you never have to guess.

Picking the tier before the number

Start from the plan you would take anyway. If AED 188 to 300 a month is your range, choose from Silver and you will have thousands of options. If your line is a business line around AED 500, look at Gold first; the number is free at that plan. Only look at Platinum if unlimited calls or the number itself is the reason you are buying. Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Gold and Platinum numbers?

Pattern and plan. Gold numbers are ordered patterns such as straight runs and mirrors, issued with the AED 500 a month plan with 3,000 local minutes. Platinum numbers are triples, quads and one-or-two-digit lines, far scarcer, issued with the AED 1,000 a month plan with unlimited local calls.

Is a Silver number really free?

Yes. A Silver number comes at no extra charge with any Etisalat postpaid plan from AED 188 a month. You pay the plan, plus 5% VAT, and nothing for the number.

Can I put a Platinum number on a cheaper plan?

No. Each tier is issued with its own plan: Silver from AED 188, Gold with the AED 500 plan, Platinum with the AED 1,000 plan. The plan is what carries the graded number.

Does the prefix (050, 054, 056) affect the tier?

No. The tier is decided by the seven subscriber digits after the prefix. A 054 quad ending is Platinum exactly like a 050 one.

Which tier is best for a business number?

Gold for most businesses: the patterns are visibly ordered on a sign or card, and the AED 500 plan it comes with carries 3,000 local minutes. Silver is the right choice for a personal line or a tight budget; Platinum when the number itself is the statement.

Are these tiers prepaid or postpaid?

All three are monthly postpaid plans, not prepaid. Prices are Etisalat's, exclude 5% VAT, and run on a 12 to 24 month term.
