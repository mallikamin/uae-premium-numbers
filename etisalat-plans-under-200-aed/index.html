The real choice: a speed cap or an allowance

This is the decision that matters, and it comes before Local or Flexi.

Plan 250 caps speed. Data keeps flowing all month at up to 3 Mbps, however much you use. It suits people who are on wifi most of the day and want mobile data that simply never stops, and it suits a child's line because it cannot overrun.

New Freedom 260 caps volume. You get 20 GB instead of non-stop data. Spread over a month that is about 670 MB a day, which covers maps, messaging, calls and some video. It is the better fit if your use comes in bursts rather than a steady trickle. If you regularly need more than 20 GB without a speed cap, the AED 300 Unlimited Data 500 plan is where the cap comes off entirely.

Local or Flexi at this price

Local minutes reach UAE numbers only, and you get about twice as many. Flexi minutes reach any country. On Plan 250 that is 1,000 local against 500 flexi. On New Freedom 260 it is 1,200 local against 600 flexi.

The standout under AED 200 is New Freedom 260 Flexi. Its preferred number gets unlimited calls, so a parent or partner abroad can be called for as long as you like, every day, for AED 7 a month more than the cheapest plan. If your calls go to several people in one country, the India and Pakistan guides explain when Unlimited 1 Country 325 is worth the step up.

Which one, by the way you use a phone

  • On wifi most of the day, calls within the UAE: Plan 250 Local, AED 188.
  • A few calls abroad a month, to different people: Plan 250 Flexi, AED 188.
  • One person abroad you call daily: New Freedom 260 Flexi, AED 195.
  • Lots of UAE calls, out and about, maps and the odd hotspot: New Freedom 260 Local, AED 195.

Just above AED 200

If none of the four quite fits, the next rungs are close. Plan 325 at AED 228 lifts the speed cap to 10 Mbps and adds 1 GB of roaming. New Freedom 375 at AED 263 goes to 50 Mbps with 5 GB of roaming. Unlimited Data 500 at AED 300 removes the cap and brings 10 GB of roaming. The full ladder is on the postpaid plans page.
