Family groups of up to 10 lines

Etisalat's family arrangement links ordinary lines under one primary account. For UAE nationals the group can reach 10 members, twice the expat limit. The head needs a Freedom plan of AED 200 or more, members need AED 100 or more, at least 2 lines have to stay active, and the group shares 10 GB of free local data a month. The full rules are in our family mobile plans guide, and priced household setups are in the best Etisalat plan for a family.

A line that does not lapse with a visa

For residents who are not UAE or GCC nationals, the regulator ties a line's registration expiry to the residence visa or the passport, and a line that falls out of registration is suspended and can eventually lose its number. Nationals are outside that rule, which makes a line in a national's name the steadier home for a number you intend to keep for years. Keep your Emirates ID details up to date with e& all the same.
