Numbers also change hands on classifieds and social media. Whoever you buy from, check these three things first.

Reading a number's pattern

Price follows scarcity. A number with one repeated pair is easy to find; a number ending in four identical digits is not, and the network only has so many of them. When you compare two candidates, count the different digits in the number and look at the last four. Fewer different digits and a longer identical tail usually mean a higher tier.
