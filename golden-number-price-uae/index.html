There is no price tag on the digits. An Etisalat golden number is issued with a monthly postpaid plan, and the plan is the price. Here is what each tier costs in 2026, what the money buys, and the one case where a number carries a premium of its own.

Why the number has no separate price

Etisalat allocates memorable numbers through its postpaid plans. The scarcer the pattern, the higher the plan it is attached to. That is the whole pricing model: you are not buying digits, you are taking a plan that carries a graded number. It also means the price is the same whether you buy through us or walk into a store. The difference with an authorized dealer is speed and choice: you pick from a live catalogue of thousands, reserve in one message, and the SIM comes to you.

When a golden number does carry its own price

A handful of numbers on our books are exceptional enough that Etisalat attaches a one-time premium on top of the plan: lines made of a single repeated digit, perfectly ascending seven-digit runs, and a few culturally loaded endings. On our books these one-time premiums have ranged from a few hundred dirhams to five figures for the rarest lines. Two rules apply. The premium is quoted per number before you commit, never after. And it is Etisalat's price, passed through as is.

What changes the monthly cost

  • The plan, not the number. A Silver number on the AED 228 plan costs AED 228 a month. Same number, different plan, different price.
  • Local versus Flexi. Most plans come in a Local variant and a Flexi variant with international minutes. The number is free on both.
  • Family lines. Extra lines on a family plan can each take a Silver number at no extra charge.
  • VAT. 5% on the monthly amount, as with every telecom bill in the UAE.

Platinum is a statement, and its buyers usually already wanted the unlimited-calls plan.
