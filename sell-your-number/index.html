Selling a prepaid number — FAQ

How do I sell my Etisalat or du prepaid number?

Fill in the short form above with your name, contact number, the prepaid number you want to sell and your asking price, then confirm on WhatsApp. We review the listing, verify interested buyers, and contact you to complete the sale.

Is my number shown publicly?

No. When a listing is published in our public gallery the number is shown masked (for example 056•••••99). Your name, phone and email are never shown publicly.

How do buyers contact me?

Buyers never contact you directly. They register interest with us, we verify and negotiate, and only then do we connect a serious, verified buyer — so you avoid spam and time-wasters.

Can I sell both Etisalat and du prepaid numbers?

Yes. You can list either an Etisalat (e&) or a du prepaid number for sale. Choose the operator in the form so we can match it with the right buyers.

How much is my prepaid number worth?

It depends on the pattern — repeating digits, sequences and easy-to-remember numbers command the most. Set your asking price, or leave it open and we will advise a fair market price.
