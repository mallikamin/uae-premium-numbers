Three checks before you list

  1. The line is in your name. Only the registered holder can ask the operator to transfer a number, so a line registered to a relative or a former employer has to be sorted out first.
  2. It is live and inside its subscription period. A number cannot be transferred once the line has lapsed. If yours has been suspended, check how long you have before the number can be reused before you advertise it.
  3. Nothing is owed on it. An unpaid balance or a commitment still running can hold up the transfer. Settle the account first.

The full rules, and what the buyer has to bring, are in our number ownership transfer guide.

What sets the price

Buyers pay for numbers they can read and remember at a glance. The fewer different digits there are in the last seven, the stronger the number: one digit repeated, two digits alternating, a pair that mirrors, a straight run, or a round block of zeros at the end. A number with a single standout feature sells; one where the whole tail is patterned sells for much more. To see how the market prices patterns, compare the numbers already listed with the Silver, Gold and Platinum tiers in our golden number price guide.

Questions sellers ask

How do I sell my prepaid number?

Fill in the listing form with your name, a contact number, the number you are selling, its operator and your asking price, then confirm on WhatsApp. We review the listing, publish it once approved, and bring you buyers we have already screened.

Will my number be shown publicly?

Yes, the number itself is shown in full on the prepaid listings page, because buyers need to see exactly what they are paying for. Your name, phone number and email are never published.

Will buyers contact me directly?

No. Buyers register their interest with us. We screen them and agree terms, and only then introduce a buyer who is ready to go ahead.

Can I sell a du number as well as an Etisalat one?

Yes, both. Choose the operator in the form so the listing shows it correctly.

How much can I ask for my number?

Patterns sell: repeated digits, mirrored pairs, runs and round endings. Enter your own price, or leave it blank for best offer and we will suggest one based on what similar numbers are listed for.
