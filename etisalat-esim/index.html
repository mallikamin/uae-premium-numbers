Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get an Etisalat eSIM with a VIP number?

Yes. Pick a Silver, Gold or Platinum number, pair it with a plan, and we set the line up as an eSIM you activate in the e& UAE app, with no physical SIM.

How do I convert my existing SIM to eSIM?

In the e& UAE app: log in with Facial Recognition, tap Manage, pick the SIM, open SIM card, choose Replace SIM / Switch to eSIM and set the type to eSIM. The eSIM activates in about 3 to 5 minutes, same number.

Which phones support an Etisalat eSIM?

iPhone XR and newer, Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer, Google Pixel 3 and newer, and selected Huawei, Honor, Xiaomi, OPPO, Vivo and OnePlus models. Send your exact model and we confirm.

Does the eSIM cost more than a physical SIM plan?

No. The eSIM only changes how the line is delivered. Plan price, allowances and the free VIP number are the same.
