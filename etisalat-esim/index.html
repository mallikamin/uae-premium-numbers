Check your phone first

Not every phone takes an eSIM, and the same model can differ by the market it was sold in. The quickest test works on any phone: dial *#06#. If the screen shows an EID (a long number labelled EID) alongside the IMEI, the phone has an eSIM chip. No EID, no eSIM.

You can also look in settings. On an iPhone, go to Settings, then Mobile Data or Cellular, and look for Add eSIM. On most Android phones it sits under Settings, Connections or Network, then SIM manager, as Add eSIM. If the option is missing, send us the exact model and we will check before anything is set up.

Two lines on one phone

Most phones with an eSIM also keep a physical SIM slot, or hold more than one eSIM, so two lines can run side by side. That is how people carry a work number and a personal number without a second handset, or keep a home-country SIM active next to their UAE line. Frequently asked questions

Does Etisalat support eSIM?

Yes. Every Etisalat postpaid plan can be set up as an eSIM, with the same price, allowances and free number tier as a physical SIM.

Can I get a golden number on an eSIM?

Yes. The free number comes with the plan: Silver from AED 188 a month, Gold with the AED 500 Gold plans and Platinum with the AED 1,000 Platinum plans. The SIM format makes no difference.

How do I switch my Etisalat SIM to an eSIM?

Existing customers can switch a physical SIM to an eSIM in the e& UAE app and keep the same number and plan. If the app route does not work for you, message us and we will walk you through it.

How do I know if my phone supports eSIM?

Dial *#06#. If the phone shows an EID as well as the IMEI, it has an eSIM chip. You can also look for an Add eSIM option in the mobile or SIM settings.

Does a plan cost more as an eSIM?

No. The plan price, data, minutes and number tier are the same as on a physical SIM.

Can a tourist get an Etisalat postpaid eSIM?

No. A postpaid line needs an original Emirates ID. Short-stay visitors should look at prepaid visitor options or a travel data eSIM.
