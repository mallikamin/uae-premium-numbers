How mobile number numerology works

The method is arithmetic. Add every digit of the number and keep reducing until a single digit is left. 050 123 4567 sums to 33, and 3 + 3 gives 6, so its root is 6. Your own ruling number comes from your day of birth the same way: born on the 23rd, 2 + 3, ruling number 5. A number is considered lucky when its root is one of the numbers friendly to your ruling number, and unlucky when it is one to avoid.
