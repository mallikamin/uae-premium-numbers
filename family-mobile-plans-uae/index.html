Family Mobile Plans in the UAE, Several Lines, One Account

The UAE family arrangement is not a single product, it is several postpaid lines linked under one account with a shared data pool. Here are the rules, and what a real household pays.

Short answer

How does a family mobile plan work in the UAE? You do not buy a single family product. You put several ordinary postpaid lines on one account. One person is the Family Head on a Freedom plan of AED 200 or higher, every other member needs a Freedom plan of AED 100 or higher, and the group then shares 10 GB of free local data a month plus unlimited GoChat. Expat families can link up to 5 members, UAE nationals up to 10.

Most guides to family plans in the UAE just point at the cheapest plan and call it a family plan. That is not what the family structure is. It is an account level arrangement layered on top of normal postpaid lines, and the money it saves comes from the shared data pool and from sizing each person's line correctly, not from a family discount.

The rules that actually govern it

MessagingUnlimited data for GoChat messaging, voice and video across the group
LinkingMembers must be mobile accounts linked to the primary holder's Emirates ID

Two consequences follow from those rules and they decide the whole design. First, the AED 200 floor on the Family Head means the head cannot sit on the AED 188 entry plan. The head goes on AED 195 or above. Second, the AED 100 floor on members is low enough that a teenager's line or a parent's light line qualifies without being expensive.

At four lines the shared 10 GB stops being a rounding error. Allocated in 500 MB increments it can be pushed to whichever line runs out first in a given month, usually a child's, without upgrading anyone's plan.

How to split the shared 10 GB

You get two modes and they suit different households.

  • Pool it. Everyone draws from the same 10 GB. Simple, and correct when the household's usage is roughly even.
  • Allocate it in 500 MB blocks. Correct when one line predictably burns through data, typically a teenager. Give that line 6 GB and split the rest.

Start pooled for the first two cycles. Look at which line actually ran short, then switch to allocation with the evidence in front of you rather than guessing on day one.

Local or Flexi, decided per person, not per family

This is where households leave the most money on the table. Every plan comes in a Local version with more UAE only minutes and a Flexi version with fewer minutes that also work internationally. Families default to putting everyone on the same one. Do not.

  • The person who calls family abroad goes on Flexi.
  • Everyone whose calls are UAE numbers goes on Local and gets roughly double the minutes for the same money.
  • If the international calling is really to one specific number or one specific country, look at New Freedom 260 Flexi at AED 195, which gives unlimited calls to one preferred number, or Unlimited 1 Country 325 at AED 325, which makes an entire country unlimited. One line on the right plan is cheaper than four lines all carrying flexi minutes they never use.

Lines for children and teenagers

A postpaid line for a minor is registered against a parent's Emirates ID, so the parent is the account holder and receives the bill. Practical points that matter more than the plan choice:

  • Put the child on a Local plan. Children rarely make international calls and the extra minutes are worth more than the flexibility.
  • Non-stop data at a capped speed is the right shape for a child's line, because it cannot generate an overage. The line slows down, it does not cost more.
  • Keep the child's line off roaming bundles until there is an actual trip.

One bill or several

With the lines under one primary account you can take a single consolidated invoice, which is what most households want, or keep separate invoices per member if the lines belong to adults who settle their own share. The family arrangement itself does not force either.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a family mobile plan in the UAE?

There is a family arrangement rather than a single family product. Several ordinary postpaid lines are linked under one primary account. The head must be on a Freedom plan of AED 200 or more, members on AED 100 or more, and the group then shares 10 GB of free local data a month plus unlimited GoChat.

How many lines can be on one family account?

Up to 10 members for UAE nationals and up to 5 for expats. At least 2 active members are needed for the arrangement to stand.

What does a family of three pay for mobile in the UAE?

Around AED 571 a month before VAT if the lines are sized properly: the head on New Freedom 260 Flexi at AED 195, one adult on Plan 250 Flexi at AED 188, and a teenager on Plan 250 Local at AED 188. Putting all three on the same mid tier plan costs considerably more for no benefit.

Why can the family head not use the AED 188 plan?

Because the head has to be on a Freedom plan of AED 200 or higher. AED 188 is below that floor. The cheapest plan that qualifies as head is New Freedom 260 at AED 195, listed at AED 260, which also includes unlimited calling to one preferred international number.

How is the shared 10 GB divided between family members?

Either as one pool everyone draws from, or allocated to specific members in 500 MB increments. You can change the split. Start pooled, see which line runs short, then allocate.

Can I put my children's SIMs on my account?

Yes. A line for a minor is registered against the parent's Emirates ID, so the parent holds the account and gets the bill. A capped speed non-stop data plan is the safer shape for a child's line because it slows down instead of generating an overage.

Can we get one bill for all the family lines?

Yes. Lines under one primary account can be consolidated into a single monthly invoice, or kept on separate invoices per member if the adults settle their own share.

Should everyone in the family be on the same plan?

No, and this is where most households overpay. Only the heaviest user needs uncapped data. Only the people who call abroad need Flexi minutes. Everyone else gets more value from a Local plan at a lower tier.
