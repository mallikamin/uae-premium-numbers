Living on 3 Mbps

The 3 Mbps figure is a speed limit, not a data limit. The data never runs out during the month; it simply never goes faster than 3 Mbps. For most of what a phone does away from wifi, that is invisible: messages, maps, music, email, social feeds and WhatsApp voice and video calls all sit comfortably under it. Standard-definition video plays without trouble.

Where you notice it is volume. At 3 Mbps a 100 MB app update takes about four and a half minutes, and backing up a day of photos over mobile data takes a while. Sharing your connection with a laptop works for email and documents but feels slow for anything heavier. The simple habit that makes this plan pleasant: set app updates and photo backups to wifi only.
